Created with the built-in image-generation tool using each original photograph as the identity and pose reference, and `sohaib-painted-portrait.png` as the style reference.

Assets: `dist/assets/hero-painted-portrait.png`, `dist/assets/hero-painted-action.png`, and `dist/assets/hero-painted-ring.png`.

Prompt direction: style-transfer painting, landscape 3:2; faithfully retain Sohaib Mohammed’s facial proportions, expression, haircut, goatee, anatomy, gloves, clothing and original pose. Match the existing portrait’s dark charcoal and oil brushwork, warm antique-gold highlights and near-black shadows. Keep the face toward the right with headroom, dark space for the title, subtle ring ropes and softly blended edges. Preserve the opponent and punching pose in the action image. No new people, anatomy, captions or website elements.

The original photographic gallery is retained. These are artistic interpretations of the source photographs.
