# Website audit and rebuild

Source: https://sohaibboxing.com/ — inspected 6 October 2026.

## Scope inspected
Home, events, event detail, about, gallery, partnerships, sponsorship enquiry, invitation enquiry, contact, FAQs, ticket retrieval and all five linked legal pages.

## Findings
- Strong original photography and a consistent black/gold identity, but repeated ticket calls to action and similar section treatments flatten the hierarchy.
- The gallery presents nine images with limited editorial pacing. The rebuild adds varied compositions, category filters and accessible full-screen viewing.
- The original biography does not publish a numeric fight record, weight class or opponent. None has been invented.
- Confirmed event: An Amazing Night of Professional Boxing, Saturday 24 October 2026, Crowne Plaza Glasgow, Kynoch Boxing Promotions. Doors 19:00. Countdown uses UK time (UTC+1 on this date).
- Confirmed tickets: Boxing Only £65; Dinner Guest £100; Inner Row £150; VIP Ringside £250. VIP includes welcome drinks, a three-course meal, ringside table position and the published table drinks package, with non-alcoholic arrangements available.
- The original dinner ticket has no detailed inclusions. The rebuild asks visitors to confirm details with the team.
- Stock counts are not carried into the static preview because they would become stale.
- All five original legal pages say “Content to be supplied and reviewed before launch.” The redesign links to the official policies; it does not fabricate replacements.

## Rebuilt experience
Eleven pages including home, event listing and detail, fighter profile, gallery, partnerships, sponsorship, invitations, contact, FAQs and ticket access. Nine original gallery photographs plus the original event poster are retained locally. The original ropes texture is also archived with the assets.

The hero uses scroll-driven zoom, crossfade and typography movement. Reduced-motion preferences disable those effects. Ticket selection calculates a local estimate for 1–10 tickets. Booking, ticket access and enquiry forms deliberately continue on the official website; no payment provider or private customer system has been copied. Local selections are not transferred to the existing checkout, and the page explains this before departure.

## Design
Carbon #090b0c, panel #141718, brushed gold #c6a96b, warm white #f0ece4, muted silver #a3a4a1. Barlow Condensed display, Manrope body and restrained Georgia italic accent. The cream ticket section creates contrast against the photographic black sections.

## Owner handover
Before replacing the original production website, integrate the owner’s event/stock feed, payment and ticket delivery services, and enquiry processing. Supply reviewed policies. The current deployment is a private presentation preview, not a replacement of sohaibboxing.com.
